### **Northern Ghana Languages (Gur Family) - NOT IN DATABASE**

#### **1. Dagbani/Dagbanli - 3,000,000+ speakers**
- **Region:** Northern Region
- **Status:** Government-sponsored language
- **Significance:** Most spoken language in Northern Ghana
- **Related:** Mampruli, Nanumba (mutually intelligible)

#### **2. Dagaare - Major northern language**
- **Region:** Upper West Region
- **Status:** Government-sponsored language
- **Significance:** One of the Mole-Dagbani languages

#### **3. Gonja - 285,000+ speakers**
- **Region:** Northern Region, Wa
- **Family:** Guang languages (Kwa branch)
- **Status:** Government-sponsored language

#### **4. Kasem - 250,000+ speakers**
- **Region:** Upper East Region
- **Family:** Gurunsi language (Gur branch)
- **Status:** Government-sponsored language
- **Cross-border:** Also spoken in Burkina Faso

#### **5. Kusaal - Significant speakers**
- **Region:** Upper East Region
- **Family:** Gur language family
- **People:** Kusaasi people
- **Cultural role:** Essential for traditional agriculture and festivals

#### **6. Mampruli - Related to Dagbani**
- **Region:** Northern Region
- **Status:** Mutually intelligible with Dagbani
- **Significance:** Important northern language

#### **7. Sisaala - Northern language**
- **Region:** Upper West Region
- **Status:** Widely spoken in northern Ghana

#### **8. Waale - Northern language**
- **Region:** Upper West Region
- **Status:** Among most widely spoken northern languages

### **Southern Ghana Languages - NOT IN DATABASE**

#### **9. Nzema - Government-sponsored**
- **Region:** Western Region
- **Status:** Government-sponsored language
- **Family:** Akan subfamily

#### **10. Dangme - Government-sponsored**
- **Region:** Greater Accra Region
- **Status:** Government-sponsored language
- **Related:** Often grouped with Ga

#### **11. Additional Akan Dialects**
