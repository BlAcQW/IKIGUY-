### Premier Health Insurance Ghana: Products, Services, and Company Information

**Premier Health Insurance** is a Private Commercial Health Insurance Scheme in Ghana that began operations in April 2011. It is licensed by the National Health Insurance Authority (NHIA). Initially, the company operated as a Private Mutual Health Insurance Scheme before transitioning to a Private Commercial entity. Its mission is to make healthcare affordable and accessible, helping people live longer, healthier lives.

***

### Products and Services

Premier Health Insurance offers a wide range of healthcare plans and services for various clients, from individuals to corporate groups. They have over 800 service providers across Ghana.

* **Teledokta Services (Telemedicine):** This service allows clients to speak to a qualified medical doctor from home and have medications delivered. This service is available from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., Monday to Saturday.
* **Health Plans:** The company offers customized plans to suit the needs of different groups.
    * **Premiercare Plan:** Offers comprehensive coverage with access to over 95% of their healthcare provider network.
    * **Premier Apomuden Plan:** Designed for individuals whose relatives abroad can purchase the plan for them. It has no age restrictions and also admits other relatives like parents.
    * **Workers' Scheme:** A government payroll-deducted product for public sector workers and their dependents.
    * **Teachers' Plan:** A government payroll-deducted product specifically for teachers and their dependents.
* **Outpatient and Inpatient Services:** These services cover general and specialist consultations, including obstetrics, gynecology, pediatrics, dermatology, and ophthalmology.
* **Optical/Dental Covers:** Plans include benefits for lens and frames as well as dental care like scaling, polishing, extractions, and gum treatments.
* **Health Talks:** The company organizes health webinars for corporate groups and clients on various medical issues and healthy lifestyles.
* **Digital Tools:** Premier Health Insurance offers a mobile app for convenient access to services.
