Here is a comprehensive overview of Fidelity Bank Ghana, including its history, mission, products, and services.

***

### Company Information
Fidelity Bank Ghana is a commercial bank that started as a Discount House in October 1998. It was granted a Universal Banking License on June 28, 2006, making it the 22nd bank to be licensed by the Bank of Ghana. It is the largest privately owned Ghanaian bank and the fifth-largest bank by assets.

* **Mission:** To become a top 3 bank in Ghana by 2024 by focusing on key performance indicators and a customer-centric culture.
* **Headquarters:** Ridge Towers, 10 Ambassadorial Enclave, West Ridge, Accra.
* **Leadership:** James Reynolds Baiden (Chairman) and Julian Opuni (Managing Director).

***

### Products and Services
Fidelity Bank offers a wide range of personal, SME, and corporate banking solutions.

**Personal Banking**
* **Accounts:** A variety of accounts, including the **Fidelity Reserve Account** for savings, a **Prestige Current Account** for exclusive services, and the **Fidelity Smart Account** for low-KYC banking.
* **Loans:** Personal loans, home loans, and mortgages.
* **Cards:** Debit cards and the **Fidelity Naira Card** for international spending.
* **Remittance Services:** Allows clients and non-clients to receive money from across the world via partners like Ria, Western Union, and Moneygram.

**SME and Corporate Banking**
* **Loans:** A range of loan products, including the **Bridge in Agric program**, in partnership with the Mastercard Foundation, which has disbursed millions to SMEs in the agricultural sector.
* **Investment Banking:** Through its subsidiary **Fidelity Securities Limited (FSL)**, the bank offers advisory services, securities issuance, and portfolio management.

**Digital Services**
* **Fidelity Mobile App:** A mobile app for banking on the go.
* **USSD (*776#):** A digital banking service accessible on any phone, not just smartphones.
* **WhatsApp Banking:** The virtual assistant "IVY" provides instant banking services on WhatsApp.
