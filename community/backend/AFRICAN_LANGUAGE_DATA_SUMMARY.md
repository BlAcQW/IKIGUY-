## 🔬 **Linguistic Features Data**

### **1. Tonal Systems**
- **Niger-Congo**: Complex tonal systems (2-5 tone levels)
  - High, Low, Mid, Rising, Falling tones
  - Terraced tone systems (Igbo)
  - Level tone systems (Akan)
- **Nilo-Saharan**: All languages tonal
  - Register tone systems
  - ATR vowel harmony integration
- **Khoisan**: Highly complex tonal systems (up to 5 levels)
- **Afroasiatic**: Generally non-tonal (except some Chadic)

### **2. Click Consonant Systems**
- **Khoisan families**: World's largest consonant inventories
  - Juǀ'hoan: 105 consonants
  - 4 basic click types: dental, alveolar, palatal, lateral
  - Complex accompaniments: simple, nasalized, glottalized
- **Bantu contact languages**: Borrowed clicks (Xhosa, Zulu)

### **3. Phonetic Complexity**
- **Consonant inventories**: 9-126 consonants
- **Vowel systems**: 3-10 vowels with harmony systems
- **Distinctive features**:
  - ATR vowel harmony
  - Nasal harmony
  - Pharyngeal consonants
  - Ejectives and emphatics

### **4. Morphological Features**
- **Word order**: SOV, SVO, VSO variations
- **Noun class systems**: Rich classification (Niger-Congo)
- **Templatic morphology**: Root-pattern systems (Afroasiatic)
- **Agglutination**: Moderate to complex
