---

## 1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

**1.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all information disclosed by the Company to the Recipient, whether orally, in writing, electronically, visually, or by any other means, including but not limited to:

(a) **Technology & Software**:
   - Source code, algorithms, and software architecture
   - Voice AI models and machine learning algorithms
   - API specifications and integration methods
   - Database structures and system designs
   - Technical documentation and specifications

(b) **Business Information**:
   - Business strategies and plans
   - Financial information and pricing
   - Customer lists and data
   - Marketing strategies and plans
   - Operational procedures and processes

(c) **Product Information**:
   - KasaAI Platform and all products (StudyFlow, ChurchFlow, EventFlow, InkWave, ERP)
   - Product roadmaps and specifications
   - User data and analytics
   - Implementation details and customizations

(d) **Intellectual Property**:
   - Patents, trademarks, and copyrights
   - Trade secrets and proprietary methods
   - Research and development information
   - Third-party agreements and licenses

**1.2 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) Is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement;
(b) Was in Recipient's possession prior to disclosure, as evidenced by written records;
(c) Is rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations;
(d) Is independently developed by Recipient without use of Confidential Information;
(e) Must be disclosed by law, with prior notice to Company.

---

## 2. RECIPIENT'S OBLIGATIONS

**2.1 Confidentiality.** The Recipient shall:

(a) Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence;
(b) Not disclose to any third party without Company's prior written consent;
(c) Use solely for the Purpose stated above;
(d) Protect using at least the same care as its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care.

**2.2 Limited Access.** Recipient may disclose Confidential Information only to employees, contractors, or advisors who:

(a) Have a legitimate need to know for the Purpose;
(b) Are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as strict as these terms;
(c) Recipient remains liable for any breach by such persons.

**2.3 No Reverse Engineering.** Recipient shall not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software or technology disclosed as Confidential Information.

---

## 3. TERM AND TERMINATION

**3.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for **two (2) years** unless terminated earlier by either Party with thirty (30) days' written notice.

**3.2 Survival.** Confidentiality obligations survive termination for **five (5) years**, except trade secrets, which remain confidential indefinitely.

**3.3 Return of Materials.** Upon termination or request, Recipient shall:

(a) Return all materials containing Confidential Information;
(b) Destroy all copies, notes, and derivatives;
(c) Certify in writing compliance with this Section.

---

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**4.1 No License.** Nothing in this Agreement grants Recipient any license or rights to Company's intellectual property.

**4.2 Ownership.** All Confidential Information remains Company's sole property.

**4.3 Feedback.** Any feedback or suggestions provided by Recipient become Company's property and may be used freely.

---

## 5. REMEDIES

**5.1 Injunctive Relief.** Recipient acknowledges that breach may cause irreparable harm, and Company is entitled to seek injunctive relief without posting bond.

**5.2 Damages.** Company may recover all damages from any breach, including consequential damages, lost profits, and attorneys' fees.

**5.3 Indemnification.** Recipient shall indemnify Company from all claims, damages, and expenses arising from Recipient's breach.

---

**6.2 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ghana.
