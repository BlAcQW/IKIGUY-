## 🏆 **Major Languages Enhanced & Added**

### **TIER 1: Mega Languages (Updated from 0 to Millions)**

#### **1. Hausa (hau) - 63,000,000 speakers** ✅ *Updated from 0 speakers*
- **Status**: Most widely spoken indigenous language in Nigeria (30% of population)
- **Regions**: Northern Nigeria, Kano, Kaduna, Katsina, Sokoto, Abuja
- **Enhancement**: Added Nigeria-specific data, government usage, regional distribution
- **Data Sources**: Enhanced existing 11 sources with Nigerian-specific research

#### **2. Yoruba (yor) - 47,000,000 speakers** ✅ *Updated from 0 speakers*
- **Status**: 2nd most spoken indigenous language (20.7% of population)
- **Regions**: Southwest Nigeria, Lagos, Ogun, Oyo, Osun, Ondo, Ekiti
- **Enhancement**: Complete data overhaul with NaijaVoices, parallel corpora, cultural heritage
- **Data Sources**: 9 comprehensive sources (speech, literature, religious, research)

#### **3. Igbo (ibo) - 46,000,000 speakers** ✅ *Updated from 0 speakers*
- **Status**: 3rd most spoken indigenous language, UNESCO vulnerable
- **Regions**: Southeast Nigeria, Abia, Anambra, Ebonyi, Enugu, Imo
- **Enhancement**: Complete new entry with IgboAPI dataset, dialectal documentation
- **Data Sources**: 8 comprehensive sources (multidialectal, cultural, research)

### **TIER 2: Regional Powerhouses (Major Updates)**

#### **4. Fulani (ful) - 30,000,000 speakers** ✅ *Already comprehensive*
- **Status**: Cross-border lingua franca, pastoral culture
- **Enhancement**: Maintained existing comprehensive coverage

#### **5. Kanuri (knc) - 7,600,000 speakers** ✅ *Updated from 0 speakers*
- **Status**: Kanem-Bornu Empire heritage, northeastern Nigeria
- **Regions**: Borno State, Yobe State
- **Enhancement**: Added historical, cultural, and research documentation

### **TIER 3: Major Missing Languages (New Additions)**

#### **6. Ibibio (ibb) - 10,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: Largest minority language in southeastern Nigeria
- **Regions**: Akwa Ibom State, Cross River State
- **Significance**: One of largest Cross River languages
- **Data Sources**: Religious texts, cultural documentation, university research

#### **7. Tiv (tiv) - 5,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: Major Middle Belt language, complex tonal system
- **Regions**: Benue State, Taraba State, Nasarawa State
- **Significance**: Rich oral traditions, agricultural culture
- **Data Sources**: Biblical texts, oral literature, university research

#### **8. Edo (bin) - 3,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: Historic Benin Kingdom language
- **Regions**: Edo State
- **Significance**: Bronze art culture, royal heritage
- **Data Sources**: Historical texts, cultural heritage, university research

#### **9. Nupe (nup) - 3,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: Historical kingdom, Middle Belt major language
- **Regions**: Niger State, Kwara State, Kogi State
- **Significance**: Traditional crafts, glass making
- **Data Sources**: Historical texts, cultural traditions, university research

### **TIER 4: Regional Languages (Enhanced)**

#### **10. Igala (igl) - 2,500,000 speakers** ✅ *Updated from 0 speakers*
- **Status**: Closely related to Yoruba (66% cognate similarity)
- **Regions**: Kogi State, Edo State, Delta State
- **Enhancement**: Added linguistic research, cultural documentation
