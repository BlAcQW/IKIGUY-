# ChurchFlow Landing Page Recommendations
## For Generic Church Management System (Unauthenticated Users)

**Last Updated:** January 2025  
**Status:** Recommendations for Generic Church Management Landing Page

---

## 🎯 Landing Page Purpose

The landing page should serve as the **front door** for:
- **New visitors** exploring the church for the first time
- **Existing members** needing quick access to services and resources
- **Returning members** accessing member portal and tools
- **Potential volunteers** looking to get involved

**It should reflect the CHURCH as an organization, not just events.**

---

## 📋 Typical Elements of a Church Management System Landing Page

### 1. **Hero Section / Welcome Banner**

**Purpose:** First impression and primary messaging

**Elements:**
- **Church Name & Logo** (prominent)
- **Tagline/Mission Statement** (short, impactful)
- **Hero Image/Video:**
  - Church building/facility
  - Worship service moments
  - Community/people together
- **Primary CTAs:**
  - "I'm New Here" → Visitor information or registration
  - "I'm a Member" → Login/Member Portal
- **Quick Contact** (optional): Phone number or service times

**Example:**
```
Welcome to [Church Name]
"Raising Champions" (or mission tagline)

[Church building image]

[I'm New Here] [Member Login]
```

---

### 2. **Service Times & Locations**

**Purpose:** Essential information for visitors and members

**Elements:**
- **Service Schedule:**
  - Sunday services (times)
  - Mid-week services
  - Special services
- **Location Information:**
  - Main campus address
  - Map integration (Google Maps)
  - Branch locations (if multi-site)
  - Parking information
  - Contact phone/email

**Layout Options:**
- **Side-by-side cards** (times | locations)
- **Single card with tabs**
- **Map with service times overlay**

---

### 3. **About Us / Who We Are**

**Purpose:** Introduce the church, its mission, and beliefs

**Elements:**
- **Mission Statement** (1-2 sentences)
- **Vision Statement** (what we're working toward)
- **Core Values** (3-5 key values)
- **What We Believe** (link to statement of faith or summary)
- **History** (brief, optional)
- **Leadership** (Pastor/Leader photo and brief bio, optional)

**Design:**
- Clean, readable text
- Icons for values
- "Learn More" link to full About page

---

### 4. **Ministries / Departments**

**Purpose:** Show different ways to get involved

**Elements:**
- **Ministry Cards** (3-6 ministries):
  - Youth Ministry
  - Children's Ministry
  - Women's Ministry
  - Men's Ministry
  - Worship/Choir
  - Outreach/Evangelism
  - Small Groups/Cell Groups
  - Prayer Ministry
- Each card includes:
  - Ministry name
  - Brief description
  - Contact person or "Learn More" link
  - Meeting times (optional)

**Layout:**
- Grid: 2 columns (mobile), 3-4 columns (desktop)
- Cards with icons or images
- Hover effects

---

### 5. **Online Giving / Tithes & Offerings**

**Purpose:** Enable giving for members and visitors

**Elements:**
- **Prominent CTA Button:** "Give Online" or "Support Our Ministry"
- **Brief Description:** Why give, what it supports
- **Giving Options:**
  - One-time gifts
  - Recurring donations
  - Designated giving (missions, building, etc.)
- **Security/Trust Indicators:**
  - "Secure" badge
  - Payment methods accepted
- **Link:** → `/giving` or external payment gateway

**Design:**
- Standalone section or card
- Green or blue color (trust, money)
- Clear, prominent button

---

### 6. **Events Calendar / Upcoming Events**

**Purpose:** Show what's happening (not the main focus)

**Elements:**
- **Section Title:** "Upcoming Events" or "What's Happening"
- **Event Cards** (show 3-6 upcoming):
  - Event name
  - Date and time
  - Location
  - Brief description
  - "Learn More" or "Register" button
- **"View All Events" link** → `/events`
- **Filter Options** (optional):
  - All | This Month | This Week | Conferences | Regular Services

**Note:** This should be ONE section, not the entire page focus.

---

### 7. **Member Portal Access**

**Purpose:** Quick access for existing members

**Elements:**
- **Card or Section:**
  - "Member Portal" heading
  - Brief description: "Access your profile, groups, giving history, and more"
  - **"Sign In" or "Access Portal" button** → `/login`
  - **"New Member? Register" link** → `/register` or `/signup`

**Design:**
- Distinct section, not buried
- Use member-related icon (user, key, portal)
- Clear value proposition

---

### 8. **Sermons / Resources**

**Purpose:** Provide access to teachings and resources

**Elements:**
- **Latest Sermons** (show 3-6):
  - Sermon title
  - Speaker name
  - Date
  - Audio/video player or "Listen/Watch" link
- **Resources:**
  - Downloadable materials
  - Bible study guides
  - Devotionals
  - Links to streaming platforms
- **"View All Sermons" link**

**Layout:**
- Grid of sermon cards
- Thumbnail images (speaker or topic)
- Play button overlay for videos

---

### 9. **Connect / Get Involved**

**Purpose:** Invite people to join and engage

**Elements:**
- **Ways to Connect:**
  - "Visit Us" → Service times and location
  - "Join a Small Group" → Small groups/cell groups info
  - "Volunteer" → Volunteer opportunities
  - "Become a Member" → Membership process
- **Contact Information:**
  - Office hours
  - Phone number
  - Email address
  - Contact form link

**Design:**
- Card-based layout
- Icons for each action
- Clear CTAs

---

### 10. **Visitor Information / First-Time Guests**

**Purpose:** Welcome and guide first-time visitors

**Elements:**
- **"New Here?" section:**
  - What to expect
  - Service format/length
  - Dress code (if applicable)
  - Parking information
  - Children's ministry info
  - "Plan Your Visit" button → Visitor registration form
- **Visitor Registration Form** (optional on landing, or link to page)

---

### 11. **Social Proof / Testimonials**

**Purpose:** Build trust and community connection

**Elements:**
- **Testimonials** (2-4):
  - Member quote
  - Name and photo (optional)
  - Ministry/role (optional)
- **Statistics** (optional):
  - "X,XXX Members"
  - "X Branches"
  - "X Years of Ministry"
  - "X Services per Week"

**Design:**
- Carousel or grid
- Quote-style design
- Minimal, clean

---

### 12. **Contact Information & Footer**

**Purpose:** Easy access to contact and additional information

**Footer Elements:**

**Column 1: Quick Links**
- About Us
- Services & Times
- Ministries
- Events
- Sermons
- Contact

**Column 2: Member Services**
- Member Portal
- Online Giving
- Small Groups
- Volunteer

**Column 3: Resources**
- Statement of Faith
- Leadership
- Blog/News
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service

**Column 4: Connect**
- Address
- Phone
- Email
- Social Media Links (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, etc.)

**Bottom Bar:**
- Copyright: "© 2025 [Church Name]. All rights reserved."
- Powered by ChurchFlow (optional)
- Additional legal links

## 🎨 Recommended Landing Page Structure

### Full Page Layout (Top to Bottom):

1. **Navigation Bar** (Sticky)
   - Logo
   - Navigation links: About | Ministries | Events | Sermons | Give | Contact
   - Member Portal button
   - Mobile menu

2. **Hero Section**
   - Welcome message
   - Church tagline
   - Hero image
   - Primary CTAs

3. **Service Times & Locations** (2-column or cards)

4. **About Us** (Brief intro with "Learn More" link)

5. **Ministries** (Grid of 4-6 ministries)

6. **Online Giving** (Prominent card/section)

7. **Upcoming Events** (Grid of 3-6 events, "View All" link)

8. **Member Portal Access** (Distinct section)

9. **Latest Sermons** (Grid of 3-6 sermons)

10. **Connect / Get Involved** (Ways to connect)

11. **Visitor Information** (New here? section)

12. **Social Proof** (Testimonials/stats, optional)

13. **Footer** (Comprehensive links and contact)

## 🔗 Recommended Navigation Links for Unauthenticated Users

### Primary Navigation (Header)

1. **About** → `/about` (church info, mission, beliefs)
2. **Services** → Service times & locations (anchor link or page)
3. **Ministries** → `/ministries` (list of ministries)
4. **Events** → `/events` (calendar/list of events)
5. **Sermons** → `/sermons` (sermon library)
6. **Give** → `/giving` (online giving)
7. **Contact** → `/contact` (contact information/form)
8. **Member Portal** → `/login` (login button, distinct style)

### Quick Actions (Landing Page Sections)

1. **"I'm New Here"** → Visitor registration or info page
2. **"Member Login"** → `/login`
3. **"Plan Your Visit"** → Visitor form
4. **"Give Online"** → `/giving`
5. **"Join a Small Group"** → Small groups page
6. **"Volunteer"** → Volunteer opportunities

## 📱 Mobile Considerations

### Mobile Navigation
- Hamburger menu (standard)
- Sticky header with logo + menu button
- Bottom navigation (optional, can hide for unauthenticated users)

### Mobile Landing Page
- Stack sections vertically
- Full-width hero
- Single-column ministry/event cards
- Large touch targets (44x44px minimum)
- Simplified footer (stack columns)

## 🎨 Design Principles

### Color Scheme
- **Primary:** Church brand colors (typically blue, purple, or gold)
- **Accent:** Green for giving, blue for trust, warm colors for community
- **Neutral:** Gray-50 background, white cards, dark text

### Typography
- **Headings:** Bold, readable (text-2xl to text-4xl)
- **Body:** Clear, readable (text-sm to text-base)
- **CTAs:** Prominent, action-oriented

### Imagery
- **High-quality photos:** Real church community, not stock photos
- **Authentic:** People worshiping, serving, connecting
- **Diverse:** Reflect church community diversity

### Spacing
- Generous white space
- Clear section separation
- Consistent padding (py-12 to py-16)

## 🔄 Dynamic Content Considerations

Since ChurchFlow supports **multiple organizations**, the landing page should:

1. **Pull from Organization Model:**
   - Church name
   - Mission statement
   - Tagline
   - Logo
   - Contact information
   - Service times
   - Branch locations

2. **Pull from Database:**
   - Upcoming events (from conferences/events collection)
   - Latest sermons (if sermon management exists)
   - Ministries (if ministry management exists)
   - Staff/Leadership (if staff management exists)

3. **Fallback Content:**
   - If no content exists, show placeholders
   - "Content coming soon" messages
   - Default church information

## 🚀 Implementation Recommendations

### Data Model Requirements

The landing page needs access to:

**Organization Information:**
- `name` - Church name
- `mission_statement` - Mission/tagline
- `logo_url` - Church logo
- `contact_email` - Contact email
- `contact_phone` - Contact phone
- `address` - Physical address
- `service_times` - Array of service schedules
- `branch_locations` - Array of branch info

**Dynamic Content:**
- Events (from conferences collection)
- Sermons (if sermon feature exists)
- Ministries (if ministry management exists)

### API Endpoints Needed

```python
# Public endpoints (no auth required)
GET /api/v1/public/organization/{org_id}
GET /api/v1/public/service-times
GET /api/v1/public/ministries
GET /api/v1/public/upcoming-events?limit=6
GET /api/v1/public/latest-sermons?limit=6
```

### Component Structure

```typescript
Landing.tsx
├── NavigationBar
├── HeroSection
├── ServiceTimesSection
├── AboutSection
├── MinistriesSection
├── GivingSection
├── EventsSection
├── MemberPortalSection
├── SermonsSection (optional)
├── ConnectSection (optional)
├── VisitorInfoSection (optional)
└── Footer
```

## 📝 Summary

**Key Shift:** Move from **event-focused** to **church-focused** landing page.

**Essential Sections:**
1. Hero - Welcome and primary CTAs
2. Service Times & Locations - Critical information
3. About Us - Church introduction
4. Ministries - Ways to get involved
5. Online Giving - Enable support
6. Events - One section among many
7. Member Portal - Easy access for members
8. Contact/Footer - Comprehensive information

**Design Philosophy:**
- **Church-first:** Show the church as an organization and community
- **Visitor-friendly:** Clear information for first-time guests
- **Member-accessible:** Easy path to member portal
- **Comprehensive:** All key information in one place
- **Mobile-first:** Works great on all devices

**Next Steps:**
1. Review organization data model and ensure required fields exist
2. Create public API endpoints for organization and content data
3. Build landing page components for each section
4. Implement dynamic content pulling from database
5. Test with real church data
6. Get feedback from church leadership and members
