## 🏆 **Major Languages Added (Tier 1 & 2)**

### **Tier 1: Mega Languages (5M+ speakers)**

#### **1. Fulani/Fulfulde (ful) - 30,000,000 speakers** ✅ *8 data sources*
- **Countries**: Nigeria, Senegal, Mali, Guinea, Burkina Faso, Niger, Gambia, Guinea-Bissau, Mauritania
- **Status**: Most widespread indigenous language in West Africa
- **Significance**: Lingua franca across the Sahel, nomadic pastoral culture
- **Data Sources**: Bible translations, radio archives, pastoral recordings, linguistic research

#### **2. Wolof (wol) - 10,000,000 speakers** ✅ *8 data sources*
- **Countries**: Senegal (dominant), Gambia, Mauritania
- **Status**: Dominant language of Senegal, major trade language
- **Significance**: Urban culture, hip-hop music, Islamic scholarship
- **Data Sources**: Literature, media corpus, hip-hop lyrics, educational materials

#### **3. Krio (kri) - 8,000,000 speakers** ✅ *8 data sources*
- **Countries**: Sierra Leone (90% of population)
- **Status**: National lingua franca, unifies all ethnic groups
- **Significance**: English-based creole, post-colonial identity
- **Data Sources**: Bible translation, broadcast media, literature, cultural recordings

#### **4. Mooré (mos) - 7,600,000 speakers** ✅ *7 data sources*
- **Countries**: Burkina Faso (50%+ population), Ghana, Ivory Coast, Mali
- **Status**: Official language of Burkina Faso
- **Significance**: Mossi kingdom heritage, agricultural culture
- **Data Sources**: Bible translation, radio programs, traditional stories, educational materials

### **Tier 2: Major Regional Languages (1-5M speakers)**

#### **5. Susu (sus) - 3,000,000 speakers** ✅ *5 data sources*
- **Countries**: Guinea (coastal regions), Sierra Leone
- **Status**: Major language around Guinea's capital Conakry
- **Significance**: 21% of Guinea's population, coastal culture
- **Data Sources**: Quran translation, radio programs, oral traditions, cultural documentation

#### **6. Serer (srr) - 1,200,000 speakers** ✅ *4 data sources*
- **Countries**: Senegal, Gambia
- **Status**: Significant minority language with strong cultural identity
- **Significance**: Traditional religion, agricultural practices
- **Data Sources**: Religious texts, oral literature, cultural research, traditional practices
