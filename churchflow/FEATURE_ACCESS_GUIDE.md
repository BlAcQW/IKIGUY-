## 🎯 How to Access New Features

### 1. **Member Portal** 👤

**Desktop Access**:
1. Login to ChurchFlow
2. Look at the **left sidebar**
3. Click **"Member Portal"** (2nd item from top, UserCircle icon)

**Mobile Access**:
1. Login to ChurchFlow
2. Tap the **"Menu"** button (bottom right)
3. Tap **"Member Portal"** (first item in menu)

**Direct URL**: https://churchflow.ailabsafrica.com/portal

**Features Available**:
- ✅ **Dashboard Tab**: View your activity stats, upcoming events, recent donations
- ✅ **My Profile Tab**: Edit your personal info, contact details, emergency contacts
- ✅ **Directory Tab**: Search and browse member directory (privacy-respecting)
- ✅ **Settings Tab**: Manage notification and privacy preferences

---

### 2. **Online Giving** 💰

**Desktop Access**:
1. Login to ChurchFlow
2. Look at the **left sidebar**
3. Click **"Giving"** (3rd item from top, DollarSign icon)

**Mobile Access**:
1. Login to ChurchFlow
2. Tap the **"Menu"** button (bottom right)
3. Tap **"Give"** (second item in menu)

**Direct URL**: https://churchflow.ailabsafrica.com/giving

**Features Available**:
- ✅ **Give Once Tab**: Make one-time donations with Stripe
- ✅ **Give Recurring Tab**: Set up recurring donations (weekly, monthly, etc.)
- ✅ **History Tab**: View your donation history
- ✅ **Dashboard Tab**: See your donor stats and lifetime giving

## 📋 Quick Start Workflows

### To Make a Donation:
1. Click/Tap **"Giving"** or **"Give"** in navigation
2. Choose **"Give Once"** or **"Give Recurring"** tab
3. Select donation type (Tithe, Offering, Mission, etc.)
4. Enter amount
5. Choose payment method (currently mock - Stripe integration pending)
6. Click **"Give Now"** or **"Set Up Recurring"**

### To Update Your Profile:
1. Click/Tap **"Member Portal"** in navigation
2. Click the **"My Profile"** tab
3. Edit any fields (name, email, phone, address, etc.)
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### To Manage Privacy Settings:
1. Click/Tap **"Member Portal"** in navigation
2. Click the **"Settings"** tab
3. Toggle notification preferences (7 types)
4. Toggle privacy controls (directory visibility, contact info sharing)
5. Click **"Save Preferences"**

### To Browse Member Directory:
1. Click/Tap **"Member Portal"** in navigation
2. Click the **"Directory"** tab
3. Use search box to find members by name
4. Only members who opted in will be visible
5. Contact info shown only if members allow it
