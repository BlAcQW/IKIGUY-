## 🏆 **Major Cross-Border Lingua Francas Added & Enhanced**

### **TIER 1: Mega Cross-Border Languages (Updated/Enhanced)**

#### **1. Lingala - 40,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION - Primary Regional Lingua Franca*
- **Status**: Dominant trade language of Central Africa, DRC national language
- **Regions**: Democratic Republic of Congo (Kinshasa, Bandundu), Republic of Congo (Brazzaville)
- **Significance**: Major river trade language, military communication, popular music
- **Data Sources**: 9 comprehensive sources (music, radio, Bible, military, trade)
- **Special Features**: **Congolese rumba music tradition**, river navigation terminology

#### **2. Swahili (Central Africa) - 20,000,000 speakers** ✅ *Enhanced from existing*
- **Status**: Eastern DRC lingua franca, expanding westward
- **Regions**: Eastern Democratic Republic of Congo, parts of Central African Republic
- **Enhancement**: Central African dialects and trade usage patterns
- **Data Sources**: 6 comprehensive sources (trade, religious, educational, research)
- **Special Features**: **Arabic influence**, trade corridor language

#### **3. French (Central Africa) - 75,000,000 speakers** ✅ *Enhanced from existing*
- **Status**: Official language across 6 Central African countries
- **Regions**: DRC, Cameroon, CAR, Chad, Republic of Congo, Gabon
- **Enhancement**: Central African varieties and local adaptations
- **Data Sources**: 5 comprehensive sources (government, education, media, literature)
- **Special Features**: **Colonial administration legacy**, modern government communication

### **TIER 2: National & Regional Languages (New Additions)**

#### **4. Kikongo - 12,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: Ancient Kongo Kingdom language, cross-border heritage
- **Regions**: Democratic Republic of Congo (western), Angola (northern), Republic of Congo
- **Significance**: Historical kingdom language, cultural identity marker
- **Data Sources**: 4 comprehensive sources (cultural, religious, historical, research)
- **Special Features**: **Ancient kingdom heritage**, traditional governance systems

#### **5. Tshiluba - 8,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: Major southern DRC language, Kasai region dominant
- **Regions**: Democratic Republic of Congo (Kasai provinces)
- **Significance**: Mining region communication, traditional authority
- **Data Sources**: 4 comprehensive sources (religious, cultural, traditional, research)
- **Special Features**: **Diamond mining region**, traditional leadership systems

#### **6. Sango - 5,500,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: National language of Central African Republic, cross-border trade
- **Regions**: Central African Republic (national), Chad (southern regions)
- **Significance**: National unity language, regional trade communication
- **Data Sources**: 5 comprehensive sources (government, radio, trade, religious, cultural)
- **Special Features**: **National language status**, trade lingua franca

#### **7. Ewondo - 1,200,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: Major Cameroon capital region language, government proximity
- **Regions**: Cameroon (Yaoundé region, South region)
- **Significance**: Political center communication, intellectual discourse
- **Data Sources**: 4 comprehensive sources (government, educational, cultural, research)
- **Special Features**: **Capital region influence**, intellectual tradition
