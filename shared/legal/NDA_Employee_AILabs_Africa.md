**AI Labs Africa**, a company incorporated under the laws of Ghana, with its principal place of business at _________________________________ ("**Company**")

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Employee is or will be employed by the Company in the capacity of **[JOB TITLE]**;

WHEREAS, during the course of employment, the Employee will have access to and become acquainted with confidential and proprietary information of the Company;

WHEREAS, the Company desires to protect its confidential information and the Employee agrees to maintain such information in confidence;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the Employee's employment with the Company and the compensation paid therefor, the Employee agrees as follows:

---

## 1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

**1.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" includes all information, whether written, oral, electronic, visual, or in any other form, relating to the Company's business, including but not limited to:

(a) **Technical Information**:
   - Software source code and algorithms
   - Voice AI technology and machine learning models
   - API specifications and system architecture
   - Database structures and schemas
   - Research and development projects
   - Technical processes and methodologies
   - Shared library architecture (@shared/ codebase)

(b) **Business Information**:
   - Business plans and strategies
   - Financial information and budgets
   - Pricing and cost information
   - Sales and marketing strategies
   - Customer lists and information
   - Supplier and vendor information
   - Employee information and compensation

(c) **Product Information**:
   - KasaAI Platform details
   - StudyFlow, ChurchFlow, EventFlow, InkWave, ERP products
   - Product roadmaps and development plans
   - User data and analytics
   - Feature specifications and implementations

(d) **Customer Information**:
   - Customer identities and contact information
   - Customer requirements and preferences
   - Contract terms and conditions
   - Usage data and analytics
   - Implementation details and customizations

(e) **Operational Information**:
   - Internal processes and procedures
   - Security measures and protocols
   - Infrastructure and deployment details
   - Service configurations and credentials
   - Incident reports and post-mortems

**1.2 Presumption.** The Employee acknowledges that all information learned during employment shall be presumed Confidential Information unless clearly identified otherwise in writing by the Company.

---

## 2. EMPLOYEE OBLIGATIONS

**2.1 Non-Disclosure.** The Employee agrees to:

(a) Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential;
(b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any person or entity outside the Company;
(c) Not use Confidential Information except as necessary to perform job duties;
(d) Take all reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized disclosure.

**2.2 Standard of Care.** The Employee shall protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care used to protect their own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

**2.3 No Personal Use.** The Employee shall not use Confidential Information for personal benefit or for the benefit of any third party.

**2.4 Internal Disclosure Only.** The Employee may disclose Confidential Information only to other Company employees who have a legitimate need to know such information in connection with Company business.

**2.5 Security Measures.** The Employee agrees to:

(a) Use strong passwords and multi-factor authentication;
(b) Not share login credentials with anyone;
(c) Lock computers and devices when unattended;
(d) Not store Confidential Information on personal devices without authorization;
(e) Use only Company-approved tools and services for Company work;
(f) Report any security incidents immediately;
(g) Comply with all Company security policies and procedures.

**2.6 Remote Work.** If working remotely, the Employee agrees to:

(a) Use secure internet connections (no public Wi-Fi without VPN);
(b) Ensure physical security of workspace;
(c) Not allow family members or others to view Company information;
(d) Follow Company's remote work security guidelines.

---

## 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**3.1 Work Product.** All work product, inventions, discoveries, improvements, ideas, and materials created by the Employee during employment, whether during working hours or not, that relate to the Company's business or result from work performed for the Company (collectively, "**Work Product**") shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company.

**3.2 Assignment.** The Employee hereby assigns and agrees to assign to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to all Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein.

**3.3 Disclosure.** The Employee agrees to promptly disclose to the Company all Work Product and to provide reasonable assistance in securing intellectual property protection for such Work Product.

**3.4 Prior Inventions.** The Employee has disclosed to the Company all inventions, discoveries, and ideas made or conceived prior to employment that the Employee desires to exclude from this Agreement, as listed in **Exhibit A** attached hereto. If no such list is attached, the Employee represents that there are no such prior inventions.

---

## 4. RESTRICTIONS DURING EMPLOYMENT

**4.1 Exclusive Service.** During employment, the Employee shall devote their full business time, attention, and energies to the Company's business and shall not engage in any other business activities without the Company's prior written consent.

**4.2 No Competing Activities.** The Employee shall not, directly or indirectly:

(a) Engage in any business that competes with the Company;
(b) Have any interest in any competing business (except passive ownership of less than 1% of publicly traded securities);
(c) Solicit Company employees or contractors to leave their employment or engagement;
(d) Solicit Company customers or prospective customers for competing purposes;
(e) Assist any competitor of the Company in any manner.

**4.3 Conflicts of Interest.** The Employee shall avoid any conflicts of interest and shall disclose any potential conflicts to the Company immediately.

---

## 5. RESTRICTIONS AFTER EMPLOYMENT

**5.1 Post-Employment Confidentiality.** The obligation to maintain confidentiality of Confidential Information continues indefinitely after employment ends, except for information that becomes publicly available through no fault of the Employee.

**5.2 Non-Solicitation.** For a period of **twelve (12) months** after employment ends, the Employee shall not:

(a) Solicit, recruit, or hire any Company employee or contractor;
(b) Encourage any Company employee or contractor to leave their employment or engagement;
(c) Solicit any Company customer or prospective customer for competing purposes;
(d) Interfere with the Company's relationships with its customers, suppliers, or partners.

**5.3 Non-Compete.** For a period of **twelve (12) months** after employment ends, the Employee shall not, without the Company's prior written consent:

(a) Work for or provide services to any direct competitor of the Company;
(b) Start or participate in any business that directly competes with the Company's products or services in the areas where the Company operates.

**5.4 Reasonable Restrictions.** The Employee acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 5 are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests, and do not impose an undue hardship on the Employee.

---

## 6. RETURN OF COMPANY PROPERTY

**6.1 Upon Termination.** Upon termination of employment or at any time upon the Company's request, the Employee shall immediately:

(a) Return all Company property, including:
   - Computers, phones, and other equipment
   - Documents, files, and records
   - Keys, access cards, and credentials
   - Any materials containing Confidential Information

(b) Delete or destroy all Confidential Information from personal devices;

(c) Certify in writing that all Company property has been returned and all Confidential Information has been deleted or destroyed.

**6.2 No Retained Copies.** The Employee shall not retain any copies, reproductions, or extracts of Confidential Information or Company property.

---

## 7. NO CONFLICT WITH PRIOR OBLIGATIONS

The Employee represents and warrants that:

(a) The Employee is not bound by any agreement that would prevent or restrict the Employee from performing their duties for the Company;

(b) The Employee's employment with the Company does not and will not violate any agreement with or obligation to any former employer or other party;

(c) The Employee will not bring to the Company or use in the performance of their duties any confidential information or trade secrets of any former employer or other party.

---

## 8. REMEDIES

**8.1 Irreparable Harm.** The Employee acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to the Company that cannot be adequately remedied by monetary damages alone.

**8.2 Injunctive Relief.** The Company shall be entitled to seek temporary and permanent injunctive relief to prevent any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement, without the necessity of posting a bond.

**8.3 Damages.** In addition to injunctive relief, the Company may recover:

(a) All damages caused by the breach;
(b) All profits or benefits derived from the breach;
(c) Reasonable attorneys' fees and costs;
(d) Any other remedies available at law or in equity.

**8.4 No Waiver.** The Company's failure to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of that or any other provision.

---

## 9. TERM AND SURVIVAL

**9.1 Term.** This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues throughout the Employee's employment with the Company.

**9.2 Survival.** The obligations under Sections 1 (Confidential Information), 2 (Employee Obligations), 3 (Intellectual Property), 5 (Restrictions After Employment), and 8 (Remedies) survive the termination of employment indefinitely, except as otherwise specified.

---

## 10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**10.1 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes all prior agreements or understandings.

**10.2 Amendments.** This Agreement may be amended only by written instrument signed by both parties.

**10.3 Severability.** If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full effect. Any invalid provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable.

**10.4 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ghana.

**10.5 Jurisdiction.** Any disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Ghana.

**10.6 Assignment.** The Company may assign this Agreement. The Employee may not assign this Agreement without the Company's prior written consent.

**10.7 Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and sent to:

**10.9 Consultation with Legal Counsel.** The Employee acknowledges having had the opportunity to consult with legal counsel before signing this Agreement.

(c) The restrictions and obligations are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests;

(d) The Company has provided adequate consideration for this Agreement;

(e) They will comply with all terms of this Agreement;

(f) Violation of this Agreement may result in termination of employment and legal action.
