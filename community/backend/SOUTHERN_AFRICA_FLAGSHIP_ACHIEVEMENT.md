## 🏆 **Major Languages Added & Enhanced**

### **TIER 1: Mega Bantu Languages (Updated/Enhanced)**

#### **1. Zulu (isiZulu) - 14,600,000 speakers** ✅ *Enhanced from existing*
- **Status**: Largest Southern African language, 24.4% of South Africa
- **Regions**: KwaZulu-Natal (dominant), Gauteng, Mpumalanga
- **Enhancement**: Complete data overhaul with NCHLT speech corpus, cultural heritage
- **Data Sources**: 9 comprehensive sources (speech, literature, government, research)
- **Special Features**: Rich praise poetry tradition, government documentation

#### **2. Shona (chiShona) - 10,700,000 speakers** ✅ *Enhanced from existing*
- **Status**: Dominant language of Zimbabwe (75% of population)
- **Regions**: Zimbabwe nationwide, parts of Mozambique
- **Enhancement**: Academic research, ALLEX project, educational materials
- **Data Sources**: 7 comprehensive sources (religious, literary, educational, research)
- **Special Features**: Multiple dialect documentation, cross-border presence

#### **3. Xhosa (isiXhosa) - 9,800,000 speakers** ✅ *Enhanced from existing*
- **Status**: Second largest South African language (16.3% population)
- **Regions**: Eastern Cape (dominant), Western Cape
- **Enhancement**: Click consonant documentation, literary heritage
- **Data Sources**: 7 comprehensive sources (speech, literature, NER, research)
- **Special Features**: **Click consonants** (unique phonetic system), Nobel Prize literary connections

### **TIER 2: Regional Powerhouses (New Additions)**

#### **4. Tswana (Setswana) - 6,800,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: Cross-border major language (South Africa + Botswana)
- **Regions**: North West Province, Botswana (90% of population)
- **Significance**: National language of entire country (Botswana)
- **Data Sources**: 5 comprehensive sources (religious, government, research, cultural)
- **Special Features**: Two-country official status, historical Bible translation (1857)

#### **5. Afrikaans - 6,300,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: Germanic colonial language with strong tech resources
- **Regions**: Western Cape, Northern Cape, Free State
- **Significance**: Advanced digital presence, extensive NLP resources
- **Data Sources**: 7 comprehensive sources (religious, literary, media, research)
- **Special Features**: **Strong technology base**, extensive digital media presence

#### **6. Northern Sotho/Pedi (Sesotho sa Leboa) - 5,970,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: Major Sotho-Tswana language (10% of South Africa)
- **Regions**: Limpopo (dominant), Mpumalanga, Gauteng
- **Significance**: Part of Sotho-Tswana language cluster
- **Data Sources**: 4 comprehensive sources (religious, literary, research, cultural)
- **Special Features**: University of Limpopo research center
