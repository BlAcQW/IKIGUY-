# AI Labs Africa - Legal Templates

This directory contains legal document templates for AI Labs Africa.

---

## Available Templates

### 1. Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
**File**: `NDA_Template_AILabs_Africa.md`

**Use Case**: Bilateral confidentiality agreements where both parties will be sharing confidential information.

**Ideal For**:
- Partnership discussions
- Joint ventures
- Technology collaborations
- Co-development agreements
- Two-way information exchange

**Key Features**:
- Both parties bound by same confidentiality obligations
- 3-year term with 5-year survival
- Comprehensive definitions specific to AI Labs' technology
- Export control provisions
- Non-compete and non-solicitation clauses
- Ghana law jurisdiction

---

### 2. Unilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement
**File**: `NDA_Unilateral_AILabs_Africa.md`

**Use Case**: One-way confidentiality agreements where only AI Labs Africa is disclosing confidential information.

**Ideal For**:
- Vendor evaluations
- Consultant engagements
- Potential investor presentations
- Demo/trial access
- Service provider relationships

**Key Features**:
- Shorter and simpler than mutual NDA
- 2-year term with 5-year survival
- One-way obligation (only recipient bound)
- Focused protection for AI Labs' information
- Clear purpose definition
- Quick execution for routine disclosures

---

### 3. Employee Confidentiality Agreement
**File**: `NDA_Employee_AILabs_Africa.md`

**Use Case**: Confidentiality and intellectual property agreement for employees.

**Ideal For**:
- New employee onboarding
- Existing employee updates
- Contractors being converted to employees
- Role changes with increased access

**Key Features**:
- Employment-specific obligations
- Work product and IP assignment
- Post-employment restrictions (12 months)
- Non-solicitation provisions
- Return of company property requirements
- Security measures and remote work provisions
- Prior inventions disclosure

## Usage Guidelines

### Before Using These Templates

1. **Legal Review**: Have templates reviewed by qualified legal counsel familiar with:
   - Ghana employment law
   - Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)
   - International trade and export control laws
   - Technology transfer regulations

2. **Customize**: Fill in all bracketed fields:
   - [ COMPANY/INDIVIDUAL NAME ]
   - [ JOB TITLE ]
   - [ INSERT PURPOSE ]
   - Addresses and contact information
   - Specific permitted purposes

3. **Verify Jurisdiction**: Ensure Ghana law is appropriate for your use case. Consider:
   - Location of parties
   - Location of work performance
   - Applicable regulations
   - Enforceability in relevant jurisdictions

### When to Use Each Template

| Scenario | Recommended Template |
|----------|---------------------|
| Partnership discussions | Mutual NDA |
| Evaluating vendor/consultant | Unilateral NDA |
| Hiring new employee | Employee Agreement |
| Technology licensing discussions | Mutual NDA |
| Demo/trial to potential customer | Unilateral NDA |
| Contractor engagement | Unilateral NDA |
| Joint development project | Mutual NDA |
| Investor pitch/fundraising | Unilateral NDA |

## Execution Process

### Digital Signatures
All agreements support digital/electronic signatures:
- DocuSign
- Adobe Sign
- HelloSign
- Other ESIGN Act compliant platforms

### Required Elements
Each executed agreement must have:
1. Signatures from both parties
2. Printed names
3. Titles (for business representatives)
4. Dates
5. Contact information
6. Purpose clearly defined (Exhibit A)
7. Authorized representatives listed (if applicable)

### Counter-Signing Authority

**For AI Labs Africa**:
- CEO: Unlimited authority
- Legal Counsel: Unlimited authority
- Head of HR: Employee agreements only
- Department Heads: Up to [amount] with Legal approval

## Enforcement and Compliance

### Violation Response
If confidential information is disclosed:

1. **Immediate Actions**:
   - Document the violation
   - Notify Legal department
   - Assess damage and exposure
   - Demand return/destruction of information

2. **Legal Actions**:
   - Send cease and desist letter
   - File for injunctive relief
   - Pursue damages claim
   - Report to authorities (if criminal)

3. **Employee Violations**:
   - Immediate suspension/termination
   - Revoke access to systems
   - Preserve evidence
   - Pursue legal remedies

### Regular Audits
- Annual review of active NDAs
- Verify compliance with terms
- Update contact information
- Confirm continuing need
