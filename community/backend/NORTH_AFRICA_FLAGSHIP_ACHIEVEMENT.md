## 🏆 **Major Arabic Varieties & Berber Languages Added**

### **TIER 1: Pan-Arab Literary & Media Languages (New Additions)**

#### **1. Modern Standard Arabic (العربية الفصحى) - 300,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION - Pan-Arab Standard*
- **Status**: Official language across all 6 North African countries
- **Regions**: All North Africa, Middle East, Arabian Peninsula
- **Significance**: Islamic scholarly language, formal education, literature
- **Data Sources**: 6 comprehensive sources (Gigaword corpus, Quranic corpus, government, academic)
- **Special Features**: **Arabic script**, Islamic heritage, formal/literary register

#### **2. Egyptian Arabic (مصرى) - 68,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION - Arab World Lingua Franca*
- **Status**: Most understood Arabic variety through media dominance
- **Regions**: Egypt, Northern Sudan, Eastern Libya
- **Significance**: "Hollywood of Arab world," cinema and TV lingua franca
- **Data Sources**: 5 comprehensive sources (cinema corpus, TV archive, speech data, government)
- **Special Features**: **Media dominance**, pan-Arab understanding, cultural influence

### **TIER 2: Maghrebi Arabic Varieties (New Additions)**

#### **3. Moroccan Arabic (الدارجة) - 25,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: National language alongside Arabic and Tamazight
- **Regions**: Atlantic Morocco, Atlas regions
- **Significance**: Western Maghrebi variety with Berber influence
- **Data Sources**: 4 comprehensive sources (social media corpus, speech data, oral tradition, broadcasting)
- **Special Features**: **Berber substrate**, French influence, Atlantic culture

#### **4. Algerian Arabic (الدارجة الجزائرية) - 22,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: National language with Tamazight and MSA
- **Regions**: Algerian coast, Tell Atlas, Saharan regions
- **Significance**: Central Maghrebi variety
- **Data Sources**: 3 comprehensive sources (dialectal texts, cultural archive, radio)
- **Special Features**: **French colonial influence**, Berber substrate, Andalusian heritage

#### **5. Sudanese Arabic (عربي سوداني) - 22,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: De facto national language of Sudan
- **Regions**: Nile Valley, Khartoum region, Blue Nile
- **Significance**: Bridge between Arab and African cultures
- **Data Sources**: 4 comprehensive sources (texts, oral tradition, media, linguistic research)
- **Special Features**: **African-Arab fusion**, Nubian substrate, tribal variations

#### **6. Tunisian Arabic (تونسي) - 11,000,000 speakers** ✅ *NEW ADDITION*
- **Status**: National language of Tunisia
- **Regions**: Tunisian coast, Central Tunisia
- **Significance**: Eastern Maghrebi variety with Mediterranean connections
- **Data Sources**: 3 comprehensive sources (dialectal corpus, cultural heritage, broadcasting)
- **Special Features**: **Mediterranean influences**, Italian/Turkish elements, urban varieties

### **TIER 3: Indigenous Berber Languages (Enhanced)**

#### **7. Kabyle (Taqbaylit) - 7,000,000 speakers** ✅ *Enhanced from existing*
- **Status**: Part of Tamazight official recognition in Algeria (2016)
- **Regions**: Kabylia, Algiers region, France (diaspora)
- **Enhancement**: Literary heritage, oral traditions, media archive, linguistics research
- **Data Sources**: 4 comprehensive sources (literature, oral tradition, broadcasting, academic)
- **Special Features**: **Largest Berber variety**, resistance culture, diaspora presence

#### **8. Central Atlas Tamazight (ⵜⴰⵎⴰⵣⵉⵖⵜ) - 4,700,000 speakers** ✅ *Enhanced from existing*
- **Status**: Official language in Morocco (2011)
- **Regions**: Atlas Mountains, Middle Atlas, High Atlas
- **Enhancement**: Tifinagh script documentation, government education, oral heritage, research
- **Data Sources**: 5 comprehensive sources (text corpus, oral heritage, Tifinagh manuscripts, education, research)
- **Special Features**: **Tifinagh script**, pre-Islamic heritage, mountain culture, official recognition
