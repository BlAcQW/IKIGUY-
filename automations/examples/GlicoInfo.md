### GLICO Life: Products, Services, and Contact Information

**GLICO Life** is a life insurance company that is part of the GLICO Group. The company's goal is to provide insurance opportunities and benefits for families across Ghana, with a focus on making life insurance easier and more accessible through a nationwide presence and digital channels.

***

### Products and Services

GLICO Life offers a variety of insurance and savings products for individuals and groups.

* **GLICO Education Insurance / Enhanced Education Endowment Plan (E-GEEP):** A savings and insurance plan designed to accumulate funds for a child's or policyholder's education.
* **GLICO Funeral Policy:** A whole life assurance plan that provides a lump sum payment to cover funeral costs upon the death of the insured.
* **Enhanced Life Savings Plan:** A Universal Endowment Assurance plan that offers a lifetime lump sum cash benefit.
* **Personal Accident Plan:** This policy covers the main life and up to two spouses (ages 18-59) for accidental death and dismemberment, and it includes a cashback benefit.
* **GLICO Family Life Comprehensive Plan:** A policy that guarantees a minimum lump sum for the policyholder and their family in the event of death, permanent disability, or critical illness.
* **Travel Insurance:** Covers unexpected emergency travel costs, including medical expenses (even for COVID-19), emergency medical evacuation, loss of baggage, and trip cancellation.
* **Group Life Insurance:** A life insurance policy for a group of people, such as employees of a company.
* **Mortgage Protection Plan:** A policy designed to protect a mortgage loan in the event of the borrower's death.
